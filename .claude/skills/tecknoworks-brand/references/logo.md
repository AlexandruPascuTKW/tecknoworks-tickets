## Logo Composition

The Tecknoworks logo consists of:
1. **Wordmark**: "Tecknoworks" text
2. **Logo mark**: Stylized paper airplane (represents "making it happen")
3. **Tagline** (optional): "making it happen"

The logo concept is a paper airplane transforming into the colorful brand identity - representing passion, vision, and the capability to deliver results.

## Logo Versions

### A. Full-Color Signature (Preferred)
- Use on white backgrounds ONLY
- This is the primary version for all applications

### B. Color Reverse Signature
- Use infrequently
- Appropriate for dark backgrounds (dark gray, deep purple)
- Logo remains full color, reversed for contrast

### C. Solid Dark Blue/Gray Signature
- NOT preferred
- Use ONLY when reproduction limitations require it
- Monochrome version for single-color printing

### D. Gray-Color Signature
- For white or black backgrounds only
- When color printing is restricted

## Clear Space Requirements

Maintain minimum clear space around the logo:
- **X** = height of the logo
- **Clear space** = ½X on all sides

```
     ½X
   ┌─────┐
½X │ [LOGO] │ ½X
   └─────┘
     ½X
```

No other elements except the corporate tagline may be placed within this clear space.

## Minimum Sizes

- **Print**: Logo width minimum 30mm
- **Digital**: Logo width minimum 100px
- **Business card logo**: 30x9mm

## Logo Placement

### Documents
- **Letterhead**: Top right corner, 16mm from right edge, 8mm from top
- **Envelopes**: Bottom left or top right, 10mm from edges
- **Folders**: Top right of front panel

### Presentations
- **Title slides**: Upper right, approximately 1/3 screen width
- **Content slides**: Smaller version, corner placement
- **Closing slides**: Center with tagline

### Outdoor/Print Ads
- Upper right corner
- Size approximately 1/3 the layout width

## Logo Don'ts

**NEVER:**
1. Delete any portion of the logo
2. Use non-approved or altered colors
3. Place any elements in the designated clear space
4. Use as a repeated pattern or decorative device
5. Use on dark backgrounds with insufficient contrast
6. Use non-approved fonts in conjunction
7. Obscure with any overprint
8. Combine with any other logo
9. Use as part of headlines or embedded in text
10. Place on pink, red, or dark warm color backgrounds

## Signature vs Wordmark vs Logo

- **Signature**: Complete logo (wordmark + logo mark)
- **Wordmark**: "Tecknoworks" text only
- **Logo mark**: Paper airplane symbol only

For most applications, use the complete Signature. The logo mark alone may be used for social media avatars or app icons with prior approval.

## Registration Mark

Include the ® registration mark when appropriate and when the logo is registered in the relevant jurisdiction.
