# 1. Why this exists

Every week somebody asks me whether a fee is refundable or when an
organizer gets paid. This is the one place where the answers live. Where
an organizer's contract says something different, the contract wins, and
please tell me so I can update this page.

# 2. What we charge

The attendee pays the face value of the ticket plus our booking fee. The
booking fee is ours and is shown to the attendee before they pay - never
added at the last step. From the face value we keep a commission; the
rest is the organizer's. We absorb the card processing costs ourselves.

| **Item** | **Amount** | **Paid by** | **Notes** |
|----|----|----|----|
| Booking fee - face value up to €30.00 | €1.50 per ticket | Attendee | Incl. VAT (to be confirmed with accountant) |
| Booking fee - face value above €30.00 | €2.50 per ticket | Attendee | Incl. VAT (to be confirmed with accountant) |
| Booking fee - free tickets | None | \- | We never charge a fee on a €0 ticket |
| Commission - standard | 5% of face value | Organizer | Deducted at settlement |
| Commission - founding organizers | 4% of face value | Organizer | First 12 months from contract date, then standard |
| Card processing | Varies | TecknoworksTickets | Never passed on |
| Ticket transfer / name change | Free | \- |  |

Complimentary tickets ("comps") that an organizer gives away carry no
booking fee and do not appear in settlement. We don't have a way to
issue comps in the new system yet - the Lantern currently keeps a paper
guest list.

# 3. Refunds

Refunds always go back to the card used for the purchase. We do not
refund in cash, vouchers or to another card. Refunds are issued within 5
business days of being approved; the bank may take longer.

| **Situation** | **What the attendee gets back** | **Booking fee** | **Who bears the cost** |
|----|----|----|----|
| Organizer cancels the performance | Full face value, automatically | Refunded | Organizer (fees recharged at next settlement) |
| Performance postponed to a new date | Ticket stays valid for the new date. Attendee may instead ask for a full refund within 30 days of the new date being announced | Refunded if they ask | Organizer |
| Attendee asks for a refund | Face value, only if the event's refund policy allows it, and never later than 14 days before the performance | Not refunded | Organizer (face value) |
| Attendee bought the same tickets twice by mistake | Full refund of the duplicate order if reported within 24 hours of purchase | Refunded | TecknoworksTickets |
| Request after the performance has been settled | Handled by finance manually, outside the normal process. | \- | \- |

A note on the 14 days: two organizers think it is too strict on
attendees. I am not changing it until the PO and I have spoken to all
four pilots.

# 4. Settlement - paying organizers

- We settle every Monday. A performance is included in the first Monday
  run that falls at least 5 days after it took place.

- One bank transfer per organizer per week, covering all their
  performances settled that week.

- If an organizer's net for the week is under €50, we don't send it - it
  carries over to the next week's run.

- Chargebacks (an attendee disputes the card payment with their bank)
  are deducted from the organizer's next settlement, plus a €15 handling
  charge per chargeback.

- Booking fees we refunded because the organizer cancelled are deducted
  from the next settlement. If there is no next settlement, finance
  invoices the organizer.
