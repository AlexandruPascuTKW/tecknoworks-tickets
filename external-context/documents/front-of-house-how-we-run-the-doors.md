*Front of House Lead, the Lantern Theatre Company (also runs the door
crew for Northgate's shows at the Old Tram Depot). 2 June 2026. Written
for our volunteer door team, shared with the box office & finance
manager at TecknoworksTickets on request.*

# Before the house opens

- Door team briefing 30 minutes before the house opens. Check you know
  which door you're on.

- The house opens 60 minutes before the advertised start time, unless
  the show's paperwork says otherwise. Matinees sometimes open 45
  minutes before.

- Each door has a printed list (soon: the scanner). Never hand the list
  to an attendee. You will see names and ticket types only - you should
  never see anybody's email, phone number or payment details, and if you
  do, tell me.

# Checking tickets

We check tickets from the moment the house opens until 90 minutes after
the start time. After that the door closes and any latecomer goes to the
box office desk. Latecomers to the Stalls are only seated at a suitable
break - an usher decides, not the door.

If an attendee has no phone or a dead battery, ask for their surname and
a photo ID, find them on the list, and tick them off by hand. Whoever
finds them on the list is responsible for ticking them - no "I'll do it
later". When we move to scanning, the scanner needs a way to look
someone up by surname and check them in without a code.

| **What you see** | **What you do** |
|----|----|
| Ticket for tonight, not yet used | Admit. Tear the stub / tick the list. |
| Ticket for a different date | Do not admit. Send to the box office desk - they can sometimes swap. |
| Same ticket already used tonight | First one in keeps it. Send the second person to the box office desk. Don't argue at the door. |
| Name doesn't match the list | Admit - names don't have to match. We care about the ticket, not who bought it. |
| Attendee is clearly under 16 at an evening Northgate show | Ask if an adult is with them. We don't check ages for theatre. |

# Re-admission (pass-outs)

Attendees who step outside during the interval can come back in on
presentation of their ticket. Check it again at the door as you would on
arrival. At the Tram Depot gigs, hand stamps are used instead - stamp on
the way out, check the stamp on the way back.

# Wheelchair spaces and companions

Each wheelchair space at the Riverside Playhouse is sold together with
the companion seat next to it, at no extra charge. The companion seat
must never be sold on its own to someone else - if it has been, call me
before the house opens. We do not ask anybody to prove they need a
wheelchair space.

# End of the night

- Each door lead counts the ticked names (or, with scanning, reads the
  checked-in number) and sends it to the finance manager with the head
  count from the ushers.

- No-shows are normal - about 4% on a weeknight. More than 10% means
  something went wrong and I want to know.

- Any duplicate tickets or arguments at the door go in the incident book
  with the ticket code.
